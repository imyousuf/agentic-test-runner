Test: A reader can page back through older posts

Why this matters:
The front page shows only the newest few posts. Pagination is the only way to
reach the archive, and a broken "Older" link strands every post beyond the
first page.

Prerequisites:
- The blog has published enough posts to need a second page

Steps:
1. Open the front page and note which post titles it lists
2. Follow the "Older" link at the foot of the list
3. Confirm this is a different page of posts

Expected Results:
- The URL is the second page of the listing
- The post titles here are different from the ones on the front page
- A link back towards the newer posts is now offered
- Posts are still listed: the page is not empty

Notes for the compiler:
- Capture the first post title on the front page before paging, and assert
  the second page's first title differs from it. Do not hardcode either
  title; both change as the author publishes.
- The pagination links read "Older" and "Newer".

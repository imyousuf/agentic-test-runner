Test: A reader can narrow the blog to one tag

Why this matters:
Tags are the only filtering the blog offers. If a tag page stops listing the
posts that carry it, the archive becomes a flat list nobody can search.

Prerequisites:
- The blog is published and at least one post carries the "rest" tag

Steps:
1. Open the tags page at /tags/ and follow the link for the "rest" tag
2. Confirm the reader is looking at that tag's posts

Expected Results:
- The URL is the rest tag's page
- The page names the tag it is showing
- At least one post is listed
- The listing is narrower than the front page: the "Older" pagination link
  that the front page shows is not present here

Notes for the compiler:
- Tag links are ordinary anchors whose href contains /tags/.
- Count the post headings rather than asserting an exact number: the author
  can publish another post about REST at any time.
- Reaching the tag page is one step, not two. Splitting it puts a check
  between the navigate and the click, and two operations either side of an
  assertion can never be named as one shared operation.

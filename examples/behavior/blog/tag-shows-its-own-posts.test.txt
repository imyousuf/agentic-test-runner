Test: A second tag lists its own posts, not everything

Why this matters:
A tag page that quietly ignores its tag and lists the whole archive looks
right at a glance — posts are there, the page loads — and makes the archive
unsearchable. Checking a second tag is what distinguishes "tags work" from
"this one tag happens to work".

Prerequisites:
- The blog is published and at least one post carries the "linux" tag

Steps:
1. Open the tags page at /tags/ and follow the link for the "linux" tag
2. Confirm this tag's page is showing its own posts

Expected Results:
- The URL is the linux tag's page
- The page names the tag it is showing
- At least one post is listed
- Fewer posts are listed here than the tags page offers tags, which is what
  tells us this is a filtered view rather than the whole archive

Notes for the compiler:
- Tag links are ordinary anchors whose href ends with the tag slug.
- Do not hardcode how many posts carry this tag; the author can publish
  another about Linux at any time.
- Reaching the tag page is one step, not two, for the same reason as the
  other tag spec: a check between the navigate and the click stops the pair
  ever becoming one shared operation.
